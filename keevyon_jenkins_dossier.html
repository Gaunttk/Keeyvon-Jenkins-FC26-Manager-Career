The Story

Born in Olathe, Kansas on January 13, 1987, Keevyon Darius Jenkins never looked like a typical football prodigy from the American heartland. But from the moment he arrived at Sporting Kansas City's Academy at age ten, it was obvious something was different. A Central Attacking Midfielder with rare vision, technical precision, and an engine that never quit, Jenkins became the standard-bearer for a generation of American technical players before a brutal knee injury — suffered in a Europa League qualifier at 27 — cut short a career still very much in its prime.

Now, back in Welsh football for the first time since his Swansea City days, Jenkins arrives at Wrexham AFC as the club's new Head Coach — bringing Hollywood levels of expectation, a continental coaching pedigree, and a point to prove.

"I fell in love with Wales during my Swansea years. When Ryan and Rob called, I didn't think twice. This club has unfinished business."

— Keevyon Jenkins, on joining Wrexham AFC
