Summer Window — Priority Gaps
Priority 1 · Central Midfield (U22)
H. Ashfield (19, OVR 58) is the only under-20 central midfielder at the club, now back at Wrexham after his Cheltenham Town loan (0 apps during the spell) and being assessed for first-team involvement. M. James (33) was sold to Middlesbrough back in Aug 2025 — historically the experienced rotation (Sheaf, O'Brien, Rathbone) aged out together with no young replacement. Rathbone (28) was later sold to Coventry City in Jan 2026, thinning the veteran count further. Damián Bobadilla (24, OVR 74, Crucial) signed as a free agent Feb 2026 and scored on debut — a genuine dynamic CM/CDM addition, though at 24 he doesn't solve the under-20 gap; Ashfield's return makes him the club's only real answer there, and he needs minutes to prove it.
Priority 2 · Striker (U21)
Kieffer Moore (33) is in active OVR decline, and Jay Rodriguez (36) has since departed as a free agent to Stade Brestois 29 when his contract expired. Cadamarteri (20, OVR 67) and Faal (23, on loan) are promising but neither is proven at this level. Chido Obi (18, OVR 68) has signed from Man Utd's academy — the high-ceiling young striker this gap has been flagging, and now ranks above Cadamarteri in the striker pecking order, though still very raw and a Rotation-level squad piece for now, not an immediate answer. MD16 vs Ipswich (0–1) — Wrexham created nothing in the final third; underlines how exposed this squad is when Moore is off the pace.
Priority 3 · CAM (U21)
Windass sold to Wolves — gap now live, not theoretical. Santi Ortega (18) promoted from the academy Mar 2026 as a dynamic CAM/LW prospect and debuted with an assist vs Hull City, but he's still raw (63 OVR, Def 35) and not a like-for-like replacement for the creative output Windass provided. Brian Gutiérrez (22, OVR 73, Important) is the senior CAM/CM/LM option stepping into the first-team gap in the meantime.
Priority 4 · Right Back ⚠ Immediate Hole
Kaboré's Man City loan ended and the club chose not to pursue a permanent deal — he has left the club. This is no longer succession planning; it's an active, immediate hole. Amelia (21, OVR 70) is now the only recognized senior RB and is unproven as a long-term starter, while A. James (20, OVR 56) is a raw dual-pos CB/RB prospect, not ready to start. There is no fallback if Amelia is injured or suspended — recruiting a senior RB should be a priority this window.
Housekeeping · Immediate Decision
A. James (20) — contract is down to 1 month; the club has decided NOT to renew and he will leave as a free agent when it expires (he has also submitted a transfer request). OVR 56, dual-pos CB/RB prospect — worth a late change of mind given how thin RB depth now is, but as of writing this is the confirmed plan. Also: Kaboré and Jay Rodriguez have both already departed this window — Kaboré's loan ended with no permanent deal pursued, and Rodriguez left as a free agent for Stade Brestois 29 when his contract expired.
