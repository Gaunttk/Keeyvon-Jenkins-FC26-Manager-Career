Summer Window — Priority Gaps
Priority 1 · Central Midfield (U22)
The club now has no under-20 central midfielder — H. Ashfield, previously the only one, was sold to Silkeborg IF on 2026-08-16. M. James (33) was sold to Middlesbrough back in Aug 2025 — historically the experienced rotation (Sheaf, O'Brien, Rathbone) aged out together with no young replacement. Rathbone (28) was later sold to Coventry City in Jan 2026, thinning the veteran count further. Damián Bobadilla (24, OVR 74, Crucial) signed as a free agent Feb 2026 and scored on debut — a genuine dynamic CM/CDM addition, though at 24 he doesn't solve the under-20 gap, which is now fully open again. Sheaf himself was sold to Sunderland (£8.4m) on 2026-08-24, with Bobadilla stepping into his starting CDM spot.
Priority 2 · Striker (U21)
Kieffer Moore (33) is in active OVR decline, and Jay Rodriguez (36) has since departed as a free agent to Stade Brestois 29 when his contract expired. Cadamarteri (20, OVR 67) and Faal (23, back from loan Aug 2026) are promising but neither is proven at this level. Chido Obi (18, OVR 68) has signed from Man Utd's academy — the high-ceiling young striker this gap has been flagging, and now ranks above Cadamarteri in the striker pecking order, though still very raw and a Rotation-level squad piece for now, not an immediate answer. MD16 vs Ipswich (0–1) — Wrexham created nothing in the final third; underlines how exposed this squad is when Moore is off the pace.
Priority 3 · CAM (U21)
Windass sold to Wolves — gap now live, not theoretical. Santi Ortega (18) promoted from the academy Mar 2026 as a dynamic CAM/LW prospect and debuted with an assist vs Hull City, but he's still raw (63 OVR, Def 35) and not a like-for-like replacement for the creative output Windass provided. Brian Gutiérrez (22, OVR 73, Important) is the senior CAM/CM/LM option stepping into the first-team gap in the meantime.
Priority 4 · Right Back — Resolved
Elijah Dijkstra (20, OVR 69) signed from Sunderland for $3.7m on 2026-07-25, giving Amelia (22, OVR 70) genuine senior competition at RB for the first time since Kaboré's loan ended without a permanent deal. Dijkstra is also comfortable at LB/CM, adding useful squad flexibility. A. James (20, OVR 56) remains a raw dual-pos CB/RB prospect further down the pecking order and is not renewing his contract regardless. Liberato Cacace's transition to RB (2026-08-15) has since made this the deepest position on the pitch — he now slots in as the new starter (OVR 74) ahead of Amelia, Dijkstra, and Barbieri.
Priority 5 · Left Back — New Gap
Cacace (25, OVR 74) transitioned to RB on 2026-08-15, and George Thomason departed for Club Brugges ($4.1m) the same day — between them the club's entire senior LB depth is gone in one window. Jorthy Mokio (18, OVR 73, LB/CDM/CM) is now the only recognised left-back on the books, and he's a converted dual-position player rather than a specialist. No natural like-for-like signing has come in yet; this is the clearest hole left by the club's youth-first summer.
Housekeeping · Immediate Decision
A. James (20) — contract is down to 1 month; the club has decided NOT to renew and he will leave as a free agent when it expires (he has also submitted a transfer request). OVR 56, dual-pos CB/RB prospect — with Dijkstra now signed as senior RB depth, there's less urgency to reconsider, and as of writing this is the confirmed plan. Also: Kaboré and Jay Rodriguez have both already departed this window — Kaboré's loan ended with no permanent deal pursued, and Rodriguez left as a free agent for Stade Brestois 29 when his contract expired.
