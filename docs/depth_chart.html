Priority 2 · Striker (U21)
Kieffer Moore (33) and Jay Rodriguez (36) are both in active OVR decline. Cadamarteri (20) and Faal (23, on loan) are promising but neither is proven at this level. Chido Obi (18, OVR 67) has agreed terms to join from Man Utd for $2.95m — deal completes when the transfer window opens, not yet at the club — which would be the high-ceiling young striker this gap has been flagging. MD16 vs Ipswich (0–1) — Wrexham created nothing in the final third; underlines how exposed this squad is when Moore is off the pace.
Priority 3 · CAM (U21)
Windass sold to Wolves — gap now live, not theoretical. Santi Ortega (18) promoted from the academy Mar 2026 as a dynamic CAM/LW prospect and debuted with an assist vs Hull City, but he's still raw (63 OVR, Def 35) and not a like-for-like replacement for the creative output Windass provided. Brian Gutiérrez (22, OVR 73, Important) is the senior CAM/CM/LM option stepping into the first-team gap in the meantime.
Priority 4 · Right Back (U23)
Kaboré's loan from Man City ends June 2027. Longman (24) is the natural heir but at OVR 68, there is a quality drop. With Barnett already sold to Watford, there's no fallback option behind Longman — either pursue a permanent deal for Kaboré this summer or bring in a young dynamic RB who can develop past his ceiling.
Housekeeping · Immediate Decision
A. James (20) — contract expires. OVR 56, dual-pos CB/RB prospect with room to grow. Cost to extend is minimal. Either tie him down now on a youth deal or release; do not let it drift. Also: Rodriguez (36) is on a 1-year deal declining fast — manage his exit before it becomes a squad management problem mid-season.
