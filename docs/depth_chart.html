Summer Window — Priority Gaps
Priority 1 · Central Midfield (U22)
H. Ashfield (19, OVR 56) is the only under-20 central midfielder at the club. M. James (33) sold to Middlesbrough Aug 2025 — the experienced rotation (Sheaf 27, O'Brien 26, Dobson 27, Rathbone 28) ages out together with no young replacement. Need 1–2 dynamic CM/CDM prospects with real development ceilings urgently.
Priority 2 · Striker (U21)
K. Moore (32) and J. Rodriguez (35) are both in active OVR decline. Cadamarteri (20) and Faal (22, on loan) are promising but neither is proven at this level. A high-ceiling young striker to develop alongside Cadamarteri is essential before both veterans are unusable. MD16 vs Ipswich (0–1) — Wrexham created nothing in the final third; underlines how exposed this squad is when Moore is off the pace.
Priority 3 · CAM (U21)
Zero youth succession at CAM. Windass (31, willing to negotiate) is ageing and approaching exit. Broadhead provides cover but is not a pure 10. An attack-minded young CAM with dynamic development would future-proof the position and let Windass exit on your terms, not his. MD16: no chances created through the middle — the lack of a creative #10 alternative is now a results problem.
Priority 4 · Right Back (U23)
Kaboré's loan from Man City ends June 2027. Longman (24) is the natural heir but at OVR 68, there is a quality drop. With Barnett already sold to Watford, there's no fallback option behind Longman — either pursue a permanent deal for Kaboré this summer or bring in a young dynamic RB who can develop past his ceiling.
Housekeeping · Immediate Decision
A. James (20) — contract expires. OVR 54, dual-pos CB/RB prospect with room to grow. Cost to extend is minimal. Either tie him down now on a youth deal or release; do not let it drift. Also: Rodriguez (35) is on a 1-year deal declining fast — manage his exit before it becomes a squad management problem mid-season.
