Summer Window — Priority Gaps
Priority 1 · Central Midfield (U22)
H. Ashfield (19, OVR 56) is the only under-20 central midfielder at the club. When M. James declines fully, the experienced rotation (Sheaf 27, O'Brien 26, Dobson 27, Rathbone 28) ages out together with no young replacement. Need 1–2 dynamic CM/CDM prospects with real development ceilings before the window closes.
Priority 2 · Striker (U21)
K. Moore (32) and J. Rodriguez (35) are both in active OVR decline. Cadamarteri (20) and Faal (22, on loan) are promising but neither is proven at this level. A high-ceiling young striker to develop alongside Cadamarteri is essential before both veterans are unusable.
Priority 3 · CAM (U21)
Zero youth succession at CAM. Windass (31, willing to negotiate) is ageing and approaching exit. Broadhead provides cover but is not a pure 10. An attack-minded young CAM with dynamic development would future-proof the position and let Windass exit on your terms, not his.
Priority 4 · Right Back (U23)
Kaboré's loan from Man City ends June 2027. Longman (24) is the natural heir but at OVR 68, there is a quality drop. Either pursue a permanent deal for Kaboré this summer or bring in a young dynamic RB who can develop past Longman's ceiling. Do not let this position fall to Barnett (25, 66) as first choice.
Housekeeping · Immediate Decision
A. James (20) — contract expires. OVR 54, dual-pos CB/RB prospect with room to grow. Cost to extend is minimal. Either tie him down now on a youth deal or release; do not let it drift. Also: Rodriguez (35) is on a 1-year deal declining fast — manage his exit before it becomes a squad management problem mid-season.
