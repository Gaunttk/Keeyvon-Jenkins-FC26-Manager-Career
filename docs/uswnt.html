The Roster

Three weeks after taking the USWNT job while staying on at the Racecourse, Jenkins named his first call-up list for the October international window. The full squad runs 23 deep — 4 attackers, 9 midfielders, 7 defenders, 3 goalkeepers — but three names anchor it, spanning all three lines: a proven frontline goalscorer, an established senior goalkeeper, and a centre-back coming off a breakout club season. This page profiles those three. Positions, roles and ratings below are Jenkins' own FC26 squad-hub read on each player; the biographical detail is each player's real career record.

Sophia Wilson

Jenkins' Rating
90 OVR

Born Sophia Smith, the Thorns' No. 9 has been the USWNT's most feared attacker for several years running — 2022 NWSL MVP and Rookie of the Year, and the player whose goal beat Brazil for gold at the Paris 2024 Olympics. In January 2025 she married Arizona Cardinals wide receiver Michael Wilson and now plays under his surname; she stepped away for the birth of their daughter and made her first USWNT appearance as Sophia Wilson in an April 2026 friendly against Japan. She's listed Crucial to Jenkins' plans — the only player in this camp with that squad-role tag — and slots into his system at ST, LW, or CAM.

Full name Carolyn Jane Campbell — born February 17, 1995, in Kennesaw, Georgia — the Stanford product has been Houston's No. 1 since the Dash drafted her in 2017, starting more than 190 games in goal for the club. She was the NWSL's Goalkeeper of the Year in 2023, has been on USWNT rosters since her U-17 days, and served as an alternate at both the Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024 Olympics without featuring. Jenkins brings her in as experienced cover in a young goalkeeping pool, tagged Rotation for this camp.

Born June 17, 1997, in Naperville, Illinois, Sharples starred at Northwestern (2015–18) before Chicago took her 26th overall in the 2019 NWSL Draft. After five seasons with the Red Stars she joined Bay FC as an inaugural signing in 2024, then moved on to Kansas City that August. 2025 was a breakout year — NWSL Best XI First Team and a finalist for Defender of the Year — the form that has put her on Jenkins' first USWNT radar. The camp's newest face here, she carries a Sporadic squad-role tag for now.
