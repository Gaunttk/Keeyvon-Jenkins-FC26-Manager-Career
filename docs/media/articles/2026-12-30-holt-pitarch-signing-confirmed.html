Wrexham Confirm Signing of Real Madrid Midfielder Thiago Pitarch

The Premier League leaders have added midfield depth on the opening day of the winter window, while last season's Player of the Year has been placed on the transfer list.

Rebecca Holt
BBC Sport · Dec 30, 2026

Wrexham have confirmed the signing of midfielder Thiago Pitarch from Real Madrid, the club's first move of the January transfer window. The 19-year-old, who can play as a central, defensive or attacking midfielder, has signed a four-and-a-half-year contract. The fee has not been disclosed by either club.

Pitarch joins as competition and depth for Wrexham's midfield rotation, having come through Real Madrid's academy without establishing himself as a regular in the first-team squad. He is the club's first permanent addition since the summer window closed in August.

Separately, Wrexham have placed striker Kieffer Moore on the transfer list. Moore, last season's Player of the Year as the club won automatic promotion from the EFL Championship, has made fewer appearances this season as the squad's attacking options have expanded. The club has not commented further on Moore's situation beyond confirming his listing.

Wrexham head into the winter break top of the Premier League table. The winter transfer window closes on 3 February.
