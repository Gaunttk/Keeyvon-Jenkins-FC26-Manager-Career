Confirmed: Wrexham Are Premier League Champions

A 2-0 win over Leeds United on the final day secures the title on 84 points, two clear of Manchester City. Here is what we know.

Rebecca Holt
National Football Reporter · BBC Sport · May 23, 2027

Wrexham have won the Premier League title, confirmed after a 2-0 home win over Leeds United on the final day of the season. Goals from Damián Bobadilla (26') and Chido Obi (49') secured the result at Racecourse Ground. Ayden Heaven was sent off in the 68th minute for a second bookable offence; Wrexham played the remainder of the match with ten men.

Final Table Confirmed — Top of the Division
1stWrexham — 84 pts (P38 W26 D6 L6, GD +55)
2ndManchester City — 82 pts (GD +39)
3rdArsenal — 77 pts

It is Wrexham's first Premier League title and first top-flight league title in the club's history. The result also completes a domestic double, following the club's FA Cup final win over Manchester City on May 15. Manager Keeyvon Jenkins, appointed in January 2025, has now delivered an EFL Championship title, an FA Cup, and a Premier League title inside two and a half years in charge.

Speaking after the match, Jenkins declined to be drawn on next season's targets. "Tonight isn't the night for that conversation. Tonight is for this group, and this town, and nobody else." Two academy players, Matthieu Brunel and Stephane Bertrand, made their senior debuts as second-half substitutes in the 69th minute. The Premier League trophy was presented to captain Callum Doyle on the pitch following the final whistle.
