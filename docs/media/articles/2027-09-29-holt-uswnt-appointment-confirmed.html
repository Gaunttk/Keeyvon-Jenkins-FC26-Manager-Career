Confirmed: Keeyvon Jenkins Appointed Head Coach of the United States Women's National Team

Jenkins will continue as Wrexham manager while taking on the USWNT post. The U.S. Soccer Federation's contract is contingent on winning the Women's International Cup within 12 months. Here is what we know.

Rebecca Holt
National Football Reporter · BBC Sport · Sep 29, 2027

Keeyvon Jenkins has accepted an offer to become head coach of the United States Women's National Team, the U.S. Soccer Federation confirmed on Wednesday. Jenkins will hold the post concurrently with his role as Wrexham AFC manager, a position he has held since January 2025.

In a letter to Jenkins confirming the appointment, the federation's president wrote: "We are delighted that you have decided to accept the offer to become the manager of our national team. We look forward to working with you and will do everything in our power to help you bring success to United States's players and fans." The federation has not yet said how Jenkins's international camps and friendlies will be scheduled around Wrexham's Premier League, Champions League, and domestic cup fixtures, though FIFA international windows typically fall outside club match weekends.

Jenkins is a 34-cap United States men's international as a player, with seven goals and eleven assists across a career that included the 2010 World Cup. He has no prior coaching experience with a women's team at any level. The USWNT job is his first international managerial post of any kind. Wrexham have not commented on whether Jenkins's club contract required their sign-off for him to take on outside international duties.

Wrexham's next fixture is away to Brighton in the Premier League on October 2nd.
