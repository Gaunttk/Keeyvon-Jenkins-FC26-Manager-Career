Wrexham Confirmed as Premier League Champions

A 3-2 win at Manchester City leaves both clubs level on 81 points with one match remaining, but Wrexham's goal difference cannot be overhauled in a single game — sealing the title in the club's first season in the competition.

Rebecca Holt
National Football Reporter · BBC Sport · May 15, 2027

Wrexham have been confirmed as Premier League champions after a 3-2 win away at Manchester City left the two clubs level on 81 points with one round of fixtures remaining. Wrexham's goal difference of +53 cannot be overtaken by Manchester City's +39 in a single match, making the title mathematically secure regardless of results on the final day. It is the first top-flight title in Wrexham's history, won in the club's first season back in the Premier League, and comes on the same day the club won its first major domestic cup, beating Manchester City 2-0 in the FA Cup final at Wembley earlier on Saturday.

The Football Association has not yet issued a formal confirmation, with the trophy presentation expected to follow Wrexham's final match of the season. A club statement said only that "the players and staff are aware of what today's results mean, and the focus now is on finishing the season the right way." No further comment was offered on the extent of Yacel Amrizi's leg injury, sustained in the 9th minute at the Etihad.
