He arrived in Wrexham in June 2025 as the quietest player in the dorms and, by most accounts, still is. Promoted to the first team on 29 March 2026, he made his senior debut against Hull City with an assist on Keillor-Dunn's winner, capping a run in which he'd already fired the academy to Youth Academy Rush Tournament glory.
