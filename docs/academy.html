Valentino grew up in Florence's Oltrarno district, steps from the Arno river, where his mother's trattoria sits on a narrow street in San Frediano. He started at Fiorentina's prestigious youth academy at age 9 — following his older brother Marco, who never quite made the grade — before being released at 14 during a difficult physical development period. Wrexham's Italian scout Gianluca Ferrara noticed him in a Tuscan youth league match for Empoli's setup, flagging a ceiling the club rated among the highest in the group (POT 72–94). His father Giorgio, an art restorer at the Uffizi, insists the boy should have a backup plan in restoration work. Valentino, reportedly, does not acknowledge this suggestion.

Juan Cruz grew up among vineyards in Mendoza's Luján de Cuyo region, where his father Hernán runs a small bodega producing Malbec wines he describes as "nothing special, but perfectly honest." He was the standout player in Mendoza's regional youth structure before being picked up by Talleres de Córdoba's academy at 13, then spotted by Wrexham scout Alejandro Ruiz during a session with San Lorenzo de Almagro's youth setup. At 16 he carries the squad's highest potential ceiling (83–94) and is the one Jenkins is most deliberately careful not to oversell to the board. His mother Sofía's fluent English meant he was the most settled foreign player in the dorms within weeks of arriving.

Ben grew up in Headingley, Leeds, in a household where football was the only religion that mattered — his father Paul coached grassroots football at Farsley Celtic for fifteen years and spotted something different in Ben at age six. He spent four years at Leeds United's academy before being released at 13 during a transitional period at Elland Road, then trained with Bradford City's youth setup before Wrexham youth coordinator Rhys Davies flagged him at a regional showcase at Doncaster Rovers' ground. What caught Davies' eye was not pace but rather Ben's aerial ability, disguised power in his shot, and an instinct for short combination play that feels several years ahead of his age group. At 15, his ceiling (POT 77–91) makes him the academy's most intriguing late arrival. He hasn't said much since joining — but on the training pitch, he has said plenty.
