Ben grew up in Headingley, Leeds, in a household where football was the only religion that mattered — his father Paul coached grassroots football at Farsley Celtic for fifteen years and spotted something different in Ben at age six. He spent four years at Leeds United's academy before being released at 13 during a transitional period at Elland Road, then trained with Bradford City's youth setup before Wrexham youth coordinator Rhys Davies flagged him at a regional showcase at Doncaster Rovers' ground.
