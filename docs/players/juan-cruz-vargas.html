Profile

Juan Cruz grew up among vineyards in Mendoza's Luján de Cuyo region, where his father Hernán runs a small bodega producing Malbec wines he describes as "nothing special, but perfectly honest." He was the standout player in Mendoza's regional youth structure before being picked up by <strong>Talleres de Córdoba's academy</strong> at 13, then spotted by Wrexham scout Alejandro Ruiz during a session with <strong>San Lorenzo de Almagro's youth setup</strong>. He carries the highest potential ceiling in the entire squad (<strong>90–94</strong>), and Jenkins has been careful not to oversell him to the board even as the pressure to play him mounts. <strong>Promoted to the first team on 8 November 2026</strong>, with Jenkins telling reporters: "This kid has the potential to be special — I want him getting minutes with the first team now."
