Profile

Nathan grew up in Brest, on Brittany's Atlantic coast, in a family that has fished out of the same harbor for three generations — his father Yann still expected him to join the boats until a growth spurt at 14 made football the more obvious calling. He came through Stade Brestois' academy before being released at 15 when the club's recruitment turned toward physically bigger forwards from elsewhere in France. Wrexham's French scout Marc Delacroix found him at the same December 2026 regional showcase that produced Thierry Besse, and both signed on 2026-12-15. Genuinely comfortable off either foot — five stars each way, a rarity even at senior level — he was promoted to the first team and loaned straight to Barnet for two seasons to get the minutes a title-winning squad can't offer an eighteen-year-old yet. Yann has made peace with the football career, on one condition: Nathan still has to help haul nets whenever he's home.
