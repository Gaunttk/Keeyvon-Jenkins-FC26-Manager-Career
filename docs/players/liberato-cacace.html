Profile

Born in Wellington, Cacace began with Wellington Phoenix in the A-League before moving to Europe in 2020 with Belgian side Sint-Truiden, then a loan-to-buy switch to Serie A club Empoli in 2022. Across four seasons in Italy he made over 80 appearances, establishing himself as a reliable, attack-minded left-back. He is a mainstay of the New Zealand national team with over 30 caps, captaining the All Whites to the 2024 OFC Nations Cup title and being named the tournament's MVP. He joined newly-promoted Wrexham in July 2025 for a club-record fee.
