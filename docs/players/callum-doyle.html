Profile

A left-footed centre-back from Manchester City's academy, Doyle captained their youth sides before a series of high-profile Championship loans built his reputation as one of England's top defensive prospects — Sunderland (League One promotion), Coventry City (play-off final defeat on penalties), Leicester City (automatic promotion to the Premier League), and Norwich City. He represented England at U19 level, scoring in the final as England won the 2022 UEFA European U19 Championship. Doyle signed permanently for Wrexham on a four-year deal in August 2025 and was named to the EFL Championship Team of the Season in his first year.
