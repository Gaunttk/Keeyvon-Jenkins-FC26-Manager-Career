Profile

Faure was born in Nantes and spent his whole childhood a ten-minute walk from the Stade de la Beaujoire, close enough that on European nights the crowd noise used to drift over the rooftops into his bedroom. He joined FC Nantes' academy at eight and was considered one of the better wide players in his age group until a serious ankle injury at fourteen cost him the better part of a season — by the time he was fit again, the club had moved on to a younger intake and there was no path back. Wrexham's French scouting network, the same one that would later bring in Bertrand and Collin, picked him up on the recommendation of a Nantes youth coach who'd stayed in touch out of guilt as much as belief. He signed without ever having watched a full Wrexham match, on the strength of one phone call and a highlight reel. His father, a dockworker at the Port of Nantes, has since become fluent in reading a Welsh football form guide he cannot pronounce a word of, purely so he has something to argue about with his son over video calls.
