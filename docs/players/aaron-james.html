Profile

A homegrown Wrexham product, James rose through the club's youth ranks to become youth-team captain, signing his first professional contract at 16 in December 2021. His EFL debut came in October 2023, deputising amid injuries elsewhere in defence, and he was part of the squad that won back-to-back promotions from the National League. To gain regular first-team minutes, Wrexham loaned him to National League North side Warrington Town for the second half of 2024–25, where he featured regularly at centre-back or right-back.
