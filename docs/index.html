The Jenkins Era

Explore the full story →

From appointment to wherever the story leads. This is the record of the Keevyon Jenkins years at Wrexham.

  1. January 2025

    Appointed

    Keevyon Jenkins leaves FC Nordsjælland to take over as Head Coach at the Racecourse, with Wrexham newly returned to the second tier.

  2. 2025–26

    ★ Championship Champions

    First full season, first title: P46 W36 D4 L6, GF111 GA47, 112 points — champions of the EFL Championship and promoted automatically.

  3. August 2026

    Into the Top Flight

    Wrexham kick off their first ever season in the English top flight, more than 160 years after the club was founded.

  4. February 2027

    Carabao Cup Semi-Final

    A youth-heavy side reaches the last four and goes out to Chelsea on penalties with the tie level at 4–4 on aggregate.

  5. 2026–27 · Current

    The Premier League Campaign

    Season in progress.
